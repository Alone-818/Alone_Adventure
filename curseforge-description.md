# Alone Adventure

**Mod ID:** `alone_adventure`  
**Version:** 1.0-SNAPSHOT  
**MC Version:** 1.20.1  
**License:** MIT  
**Author:** lieyan_alone

This mod adds a diverse collection of weapons, armor, accessories, materials, and unique quest contracts. Designed around challenging solo survival and progressive gear upgrades. Currently in early testing phase.

---

## 🔫 Guns & Ammo
- **2 Guns Available**: Double-Barrel Shotgun, Pistol
- **5+ Ammo Types**: Standard, Incendiary, Poison, Explosive, Armor-Piercing, and more
*(More guns coming in future updates)*

## 🛡️ Accessories (requires Curios)
**30+ Unique Accessories** — Each with passive and/or active abilities:
- **Broken Mask** — +7 Armor, +20% HP, +3 Toughness, applies Vulnerability
- **Crystalline Heart** — Sacrifice health for scalable shield
- **Necromancer's Ledger** — Trade health/damage for Calamity DoT and guaranteed crits
- **Sealed Throne** — Starlight resource system for damage/armor scaling
- **Charging Core** — Three elemental stances (Thunder/Frost/Dark) with charge layers
- **Hunter Serum** — Toggleable vision mode with enemy/item highlight
- And many more: Dragon Power, Imperial Eagle, Binding Bandage, Survival Whimper, etc.

## ⚔️ Melee Weapons
Powerful melee weapons with unique mechanics:
- **Power Sword** — Energy blade with overclock mode
- **Chainsaw Sword** — Rapid sweep with bleed effects
- **Reaper Scythe** — Damage increases per unique enemy killed
- **Machine Claw** — Fast combo attacks with scaling damage
- **Ink Blade** — Thrown weapons that return
- And more: Painstrike Hammer, Shock Device, Regiment Banner, Starlight Greatsword.

## 📜 Quest Contracts
Complete dangerous contracts for powerful rewards:
- **Battle Contract** — Defeat waves of undead
- **Evil Contract** — Villager sacrifice ritual
- **Imperial Contract** — Nether exploration and boss hunts
- **Resurrection Contract** — Trade rare items for revival power
- **Tower Contract** — Reach the sky and conquer the Wither
*(Each contract can be used as a crafting material after completion)*

## 🧪 Custom Mechanics & Features
- **Potion System** — Brew Endurance potions from Golden Beetroot
- **Crafting Materials** — Monster Ash, Blood Shard, Gun Parts
- **Consumables** — First Aid Kit, Eerie Music Box
- **Custom Effects** — Endurance, Vulnerability, Laceration, Calamity

## ⚙️ Technical Details

### Dependencies
- **Required:** Curios, GeckoLib
- **Optional:** Just Enough Items (JEI)

### Configuration
All stats and mechanics are fully configurable via `alone_adventure-common.toml`.

### Installation
1. Install Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge 47.4.23
2. Download and install [Curios](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/curios) and [GeckoLib](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/geckolib)
3. Place the mod file in your `mods` folder
4. *(Optional)* Install JEI for recipe browsing

### Known Limitations
- Still in **early testing phase**
- Textures are AI-assisted
- Code is experimental; balance may change
